# SIU Escalation Procedure

## When to escalate
Escalate a case to the Special Investigations Unit when any of the following hold:
- The blended risk score is at or above the high-risk threshold.
- The vendor or provider carries a prior confirmed-fraud flag.
- Anomaly signals indicate irregular payment behavior that rules cannot explain.
- The automated summary confidence is low and the case value is material.

## Escalation packet
Every escalation must include: the claim identifier, the risk and anomaly
scores, the top contributing factors, cited policies, and a recommended action.
A temporary payment hold should be requested for high-risk escalations.

## Human judgment
High-risk and uncertain cases always require senior-investigator approval before
any payment action. Automated systems must pause and request human review at
these checkpoints.
