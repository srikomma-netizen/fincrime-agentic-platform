# Vendor Risk and Onboarding Controls

## Vendor risk tiers
Vendors are assigned a risk tier (low, medium, high) based on payment history,
sanctions/watchlist screening support, geography, and prior case outcomes.

## High-risk vendors
Payments to high-risk vendors, or vendors with an open or prior confirmed-fraud
case, must be reviewed before release. Repeated small payments just below review
thresholds ("structuring") are a high-risk indicator.

## Continuous monitoring
Vendor risk is re-evaluated when new adverse information is retrieved. A vendor
flagged during a case investigation should have that finding recorded for future
retrieval by the prior-case tool.
