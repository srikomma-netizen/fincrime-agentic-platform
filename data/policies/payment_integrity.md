# Payment Integrity and FWA Policy

## Scope
This policy governs the review of healthcare claims and vendor payments for
fraud, waste, and abuse (FWA). It applies to all claims processed through the
payment-integrity pipeline.

## High-dollar review
Any claim with a billed amount at or above $10,000 must receive at least an
analyst-level review. Claims at or above $50,000 require senior-investigator
sign-off before payment is released.

## Duplicate and split billing
Claims that duplicate a previously paid claim, or that appear to split a single
service into multiple line items to avoid review thresholds, must be held and
routed to the analyst queue. Two or more matching procedure codes for the same
member and date of service is a duplicate indicator.

## Provider outlier detection
When a claim amount exceeds three times the provider's trailing average for the
same procedure code, the claim is treated as a statistical outlier and flagged
for anomaly review.
